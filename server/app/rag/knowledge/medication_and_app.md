# Medication safety

## General rules
Take medicines exactly as prescribed. Do not double up after a missed dose unless the label says so. Check allergies before taking a new medicine, and tell every doctor and pharmacist about all medicines and supplements you take. Store away from heat, moisture and children.

## Paracetamol (acetaminophen)
Typical adult dose is 500 to 1000 mg every 4 to 6 hours, maximum 4 g in 24 hours (less if you have liver disease or drink alcohol). Many cold remedies also contain it; count them in the total.

## NSAIDs (ibuprofen, diclofenac, aspirin)
Take with food. Avoid if you have stomach ulcers, kidney disease, are in late pregnancy, or take blood thinners unless a doctor advises. They can raise blood pressure.

## Missed dose
Take it as soon as you remember unless it is almost time for the next one. Never take two doses together. For critical medicines such as insulin, anticoagulants or anti-epileptics ask your doctor for a missed-dose plan.

## Warning signs after a medicine
Rash, swelling, trouble breathing, yellow skin or eyes, black stools, or severe dizziness: stop the medicine and seek medical help urgently.

# Using Jeevan

## SOS
Hold the SOS button in the app for 1.5 seconds, or hold the hardware SOS button on the VitaWatch for 1.5 seconds. Your emergency contacts receive your live location and a video link. Press Safe when you are okay to resolve the alert.

## Medicine reminders
When a dose is due the VitaWatch LED lights up solid. Press the medicine button on the watch, or confirm in the app, to mark it taken.

## Taking a good reading
Rest for a minute. Keep the finger still on the pulse sensor with gentle pressure, and keep the hand warm and at heart level. The watch reads pulse and SpO2 first, then temperature, then ECG, and the app updates live.

## Walking stick
The smart walking stick shares location and fall alerts. Pair it from the Devices screen.
